Why some absences run long: one factor stands out. The odds that a planned-care absence is long (8 hours or more) are about 13.5x lower than for illness or injury absences. Nothing else is statistically clear.

Honest reading

  • Association, not cause. Logistic model of "event is 8 h or more" on 696 events with hours above zero, standard errors clustered by employee (33 clusters).
  • "Planned care" is part of how the outcome is built (visits are short by nature), so this finding is expected, not surprising. The useful part is that age, commute distance, tenure and workload show no clear effect.
  • Monday is borderline (odds ratio 1.37, p 0.10). I would not act on it.
